Newton's First Law — Law of Inertia

An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion, unless acted upon by an external force.

Newton's Second Law — F = ma

Force equals mass times acceleration. Acceleration is directly proportional to force and inversely proportional to mass.

Paused
F = m × a   →   a = F / m
Double the force, double the acceleration. Double the mass, halve the acceleration. Try it below!

Newton's Third Law — Action & Reaction

For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. Forces always come in pairs.

Paused
FAB = −FBA   →   |FAB| = |FBA|
When two objects collide, they exert equal and opposite forces on each other. Total momentum is always conserved!

Real-World Example
When a cannon fires, the cannonball shoots forward (action). Simultaneously, the cannon recoils backward with equal and opposite force (reaction). The momentum of both加起来 equals zero — just as before firing. A rocket does the same thing: it pushes exhaust gases downward, and the gases push the rocket upward!
